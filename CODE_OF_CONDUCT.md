# CODE OF CONDUCT

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **ANA MAX** pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Be **respectful** and considerate.
- Use **inclusive language**; avoid harassing, discriminatory, or hateful remarks.
- **Critique ideas, not people**.
- **No trolling** or intentionally disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at `security@ana-max.dev`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is **appropriate to the circumstances**.

We value the contributions of our community and thank you for helping us keep this project a safe space.
